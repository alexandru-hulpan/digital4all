ERASMUS+ DIGI-EQUAL | VASLUI, ROMANIA

Day 2

11 March 2026

The second day of the Erasmus+ DigiEqual project was dedicated to developing digital skills and critical thinking through interactive activities and thematic workshops.

See gallery

Activities carried out

The day’s program began with the arrival of participants, followed by a media literacy workshop where students discussed information sources, the phenomenon of fake news, and the importance of critical thinking online. The activity provided participants with useful tools to accurately analyze information and navigate the digital space responsibly. The activity was coordinated by teacher Virginia Pracsiu. The LER EPAS team also presented information on fake news and discussed specific examples.
After a short break, the "Digital Footprint" workshop took place; here, students explored the concepts of online identity, internet safety, and responsible digital behavior. Discussions and practical exercises helped participants realize the impact their online activity could have on their personal and professional futures. This activity was coordinated by Police Inspector Alexandra Condurache.
After lunch, the participants took part in a study visit to the city of Iași, focused on cultural and educational exploration. The activity included digital research and the discovery of key cultural landmarks, helping to blend learning with intercultural experiences.
Through these activities, the DigiEqual project continues to promote responsible technology use, international collaboration, and the development of students' digital skills.
