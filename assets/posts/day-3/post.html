ERASMUS+ DIGI-EQUAL | VASLUI, ROMANIA

Day 3

11 March 2026

The third day of the mobility under the Erasmus+ DigiEqual project was dedicated to educational robotics and its role in developing digital competencies and future-ready skills.

See gallery See resources

Activities carried out

The day's activities began with an introductory session led by the RoLERbot robotics team—coordinated by teacher Camelia Popica—which highlighted the importance of educational robotics in the digitalization of education and its role in fostering logical thinking, creativity, and STEM skills.
Following the introductory session, participants took part in a hands-on robotics workshop led by student Alexandru Hulpan (class XI A), where they worked in teams to program and test robots.
The activity offered a chance to gain firsthand experience with digital tools and modern teaching methods rooted in practical, collaborative learning, as well as to see a presentation of "Puișor," a robot created by the LER robotics team.
In the second part of the day, students—guided by teacher Virginia Pracsiu—worked in groups to create the project's final product.
Through these activities, participants explored innovative ways to integrate technology into education, contributing to the DigiEqual project's goal of promoting digital skills and inclusion within the educational environment.
The Erasmus+ DigiEqual project continues to provide teachers and participants with valuable learning experiences, opportunities to exchange best practices, and ideas for an education system adapted to today's digital challenges.
