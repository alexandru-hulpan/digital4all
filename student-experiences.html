ERASMUS+ DIGI-EQUAL | STUDENT VOICES

What do students say about this experience?

The experience, told by students

My experience with the Erasmus+ DigiEqual project, hosted by "Emil Racoviță" Theoretical High School in Vaslui, was a key factor in my personal and educational growth. Beyond the role of hosting students from Poland and Italy, the project focused on how technology can ensure equal educational opportunities for young people from both urban and rural areas.
The most significant benefit was the development of practical digital skills. I learned to use platforms like Canva and CapCut not merely as editing tools, but as means of communication, creating graphic and video content that conveys a powerful message. Furthermore, through collaboration with the robotics team and participation in media workshops, I learned how to navigate the online world responsibly, protect my digital identity, and critically analyze information to combat fake news.
On a social and linguistic level, the project revealed my genuine passion for working in an international environment. Daily interaction within mixed teams pushed me to overcome barriers and visibly improved my English communication skills, boosting both my fluency and self-confidence. Additionally, I gained a truly open cultural mindset; I learned to discover, respect, and value the differences in our lifestyles, while also realizing the many shared aspirations that unite us as European students.

Rusu Ștefania

Last school year, I was able to participate for the very first time in an Erasmus+ project, which was definitely a formative experience for me, so I wanted to take part in one for a very long while.
Of course, I was nervous during the first day when the students from Poland and Italy arrived, but as we talked to each other I realised that they were quite nice.
I found the workshops to be very interesting, as we often had to work in teams and present our work in front of the other students. We also learned new information on how to be digitally literate and about each other's cultures.
This experience helped me become a more social and confident person, as I got to meet new people. I also learned more about online safety.
Overall, I'm very happy I got to be a part of this project!

Nechita Alesia

My Erasmus experience through the DIGI-EQUAL project was educational and beautiful. I learned to use platforms like eTwinning and discovered more about the cultures and lifestyles of students from other countries.
During the project, I compared Romania with Poland and learned the importance of teamwork, communication and cultural diversity. The mobility in Koszalin was a great experience because I made new friends, improved my English and became more confident.
I am grateful for this experience because it gave me beautiful memories and useful skills for the future.

Halapciug Daria
