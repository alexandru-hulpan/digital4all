Activities carried out

Storytelling & Digital Magic! Today, we put technology to work to learn the art of digital storytelling as part of the Erasmus+ Digi-Equal project.
Workshop highlights:
24 students from 🇷🇴 Romania, Italy, and Poland.
Mixed teams demonstrating that good ideas need no translation.
Intensive workshop: we mastered CapCut and Canva Video to create content with a meaningful message. Why Digital Storytelling?
Because having an idea isn't enough—you need to know how to share it with the world! Students learned to transform a simple clip into a visual experience using editing and digital graphics techniques. Coordinator: Teacher Gina Luiza Budaca. Another workshop focused on "Digital Tools for Learning English."
Students from the participating countries presented the apps they use in English classes, discussing their respective pros and cons. This activity utilized platforms such as Mentimeter and Padlet. Creating a blog (Digital Classroom Hub) proved to be a real challenge for the participants. Coordinators: Teachers Oana Petronela Ponea and Andreea Zahariuc.
🇹🇩 A festive dinner concluded the activities of the project's fourth day.
