ERASMUS+ DIGI-EQUAL | VASLUI, ROMANIA

Day 5

13 March 2026

March 13, conclusion of the Erasmus+ project meeting "Digital Inclusion for Equal Learning Opportunities" (DigiEqual) at "Emil Racoviță" Theoretical High School in Vaslui.

See gallery

Activities carried out

Today marked the successful conclusion of the working meeting for the Erasmus+ project "DigiEqual," an initiative dedicated to promoting digital education and equal opportunities for young people within the European educational landscape.
During the final session, participants completed the "Digital Education in Europe – Student Toolkit" workshop—a practical resource designed to help students develop digital skills and actively participate in modern education. The digital Student Toolkit, a product of the collaboration between project partners, was also officially presented. Activity coordinators: Loredana Toma and Daniel Hulubei.
The meeting concluded on a positive note with a certificate award ceremony and a session reflecting on the activities, shared experiences, and the project's impact on participants from the three countries involved: Poland, Italy, and Romania. 🇹🇩🇵🇱🇮🇹 We thank all partners and participants for their involvement, ideas, and collaboration!
Together, we are helping to build a more inclusive and accessible digital education system for everyone.
