ERASMUS+ DIGI-EQUAL | VASLUI, ROMANIA

Day 1

10 March 2026

The first day of the meeting was dedicated to getting to know one another and to activities focused on digital education.

See gallery

Activities carried out

The activities kicked off with an opening session where the project team welcomed the participants and presented the mobility’s objectives and the week's schedule.
Next came a meeting with representatives from Europe Direct, where students discussed the skills gained through Erasmus+ programs, European educational opportunities, and the importance of international collaboration in education.
After a short break, the participants continued with the "Digital Education in My Country" workshop; students from the three countries presented their perspectives on digital education within their respective school systems, followed by discussions and an exchange of ideas.
The program continued with a lunch break, giving participants the chance to socialize and share cultural experiences.
Later in the day, the international guests visited Vaslui City Hall, where they learned about the local community and its openness to local, national, and European educational projects.
The day concluded in an interactive and dynamic way with the "Digital Treasure Hunt – Discover Vaslui" activity—a digital scavenger hunt across the city using QR codes, digital maps, and mobile apps, allowing students to discover key landmarks in Vaslui.
The first day of the mobility offered an excellent opportunity for collaboration, learning, and cultural discovery, laying the groundwork for a week filled with educational, intercultural, and digital activities.
